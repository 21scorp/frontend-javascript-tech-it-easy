## Title
**WISP — a tiny light that grows with you**

## Tagline (short description)
Name a little light. Feed it by touch. Build it a living meadow. Raise it
into a star that watches over everything that comes after.

## Description

> *In the dark between the hills, something small is falling…*

**WISP is a one-tab idle game about caring for something small.**

A tiny glowing creature lands in your hands. You give it a name — and from
that moment it's yours. It blinks. It watches your cursor. It gets sleepy
when *you* should be sleeping. Hold it gently and it melts.

Feed it light by touch, and build it a world: fireflies, paper lanterns,
moonflowers, an owl that tells one tale per day, a beacon, an aurora, a
moon garden — every single thing you buy appears in the meadow and stays.

And when your wisp has grown as far as the meadow can carry it, you let it
go — upward. It becomes a **permanent star in your sky, with its name**,
watching over every wisp that comes after. Raise enough of them and they
form a constellation. You get to name that too.

**Kind by design.** Nothing dies. Nothing punishes you. Time away is
rewarded — your wisp gathers light while you're gone, dreams about the
things you built together, and tells you about it when you come back.

### Features
- 🐣 A creature with moods, dreams, a voice, and a bond that deepens
- 🏗 14 buildings, 25 boosts — all visible, all alive, all animated
- 🌠 Prestige with a heart: ascended wisps become named stars, forever
- 🌙 Daily gifts, streaks, owl tales (30!), letters from the Moon
- 💫 Golden dewdrops, starfall showers, comet wishes, wandering visitors
- 👒 11 accessories — earned by loyalty, never bought
- 📷 One-tap postcards of your meadow to share
- 📱 Installs as an app (PWA), plays offline, saves automatically
- 🔊 Fully synthesized sound — taps, chimes, crickets, a music box
- 💛 No ads. No tracking. Cosmetics-only supporter gifts.

**Made with zero sprites — everything you see is drawn by code, live.**

## Controls
- **Tap / click** your wisp to gather light (rhythm combos!)
- **Press & hold** to pet it
- Tap comets, dewdrops, visitors, stars, the owl… everything responds
- Desktop: **space** boops
